# Genesis 3:8 Open Questions

### OQ-3.8-A — Human anticipation and demonstrated character
Does the humans' post-transgression perception/anticipation of God correspond to the way God actually responds to them?

### OQ-3.8-B — Reader expectation
What does a reader expect God to do after Genesis 3:8, and how much of that expectation comes from Genesis itself rather than prior theology, culture, personality, or experience?

### OQ-3.8-C — *Leruaḥ hayyom*
What precise environmental/temporal force does *leruaḥ hayyom* carry? Does later evidence materially strengthen breeze/cooling, late-day, or storm proposals?

### OQ-3.8-D — Same-day chronology
Does the narrative intend the encounter to occur on the same day as the eating? Present evidence does not establish this.

### OQ-3.8-E — Covering and concealment
Did the humans experience their coverings as inadequate in relation to God's perceptible presence, or does hiding respond to a broader relational problem?

### OQ-3.8-F — Direction of divine movement
Does subsequent narrative evidence establish that God's movement in verse 8 should be understood as movement toward the humans, or should that remain distinct from the later directed encounter?
