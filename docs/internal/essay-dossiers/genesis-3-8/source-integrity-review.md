# Genesis 3:8 Source Integrity Review

Checked October 4, 2026 for the implementation of *They Hid From God*. The public references below support the limited attributions used in the approved package. No essay prose or adjudicated status was changed.

## Scope and provenance

The investigation named source families but left several editions unidentified. This pass verifies named representative editions and accessible source texts; it does not establish which unnamed editions the earlier conversation originally consulted. That historical limitation is stated in public AR-3.8-08. The new links are implementation verification references, not reconstructed historical citations.

The eight supplied full ARs remain the governed reasoning. The source check found the competing readings already anticipated by the dossier; it did not establish a new reason to alter its bounded judgments. Sources that discuss later verses remain external dialogue and cannot extend the essay’s evidence horizon.

It reproduces no unverified quotation or page-specific claim from the article or book.
