# Genesis 3:1–7 — Synthesis Dossier

**Status:** Whole-unit synthesis complete for the bounded Genesis 3:1–7 movement  
**Evidence base:** Seven frozen verse-level Working Investigation Dossiers: Genesis 3:1, 3:2, 3:3, 3:4, 3:5, 3:6, 3:7  
**Governing methodology:** Genesis Character-of-God methodology through the Post–Genesis 2:4–25 reconciliation; Genesis 3 methodological developments remain candidates unless explicitly adjudicated here  
**Hard information boundary:** **Genesis 3:7. Genesis 3:8 and later material are excluded from this synthesis.**  
**Governing question:** **What does the evidence actually authorize?**

---

## 1. Purpose of This Synthesis

The seven verse-level dossiers deliberately prevented later verses from rewriting earlier evidentiary states. This synthesis now permits the complete Genesis 3:1–7 movement to illuminate itself without admitting later material.

The task is not to repeat seven dossiers. It is to ask:

> **What becomes visible only when the seven frozen evidentiary states are placed together?**

Accordingly, this dossier:
- preserves verse-level findings and negative findings;
- tests prospective hypotheses against the completed 3:1–7 movement;
- identifies trajectories that no single verse could establish;
- distinguishes observation, strong inference, purpose inference, and unresolved claim;
- adjudicates methodological candidates generated during the movement;
- keeps Genesis 3:7 as the terminal boundary.

---

## 2. Pre-Movement Revelatory Anchors

Genesis 3:1 does not begin in an evidentiary vacuum.

Before the serpent speaks, the disclosed history includes:
- God as Creator;
- abundant provision;
- blessing;
- positive appraisal;
- relational attention;
- meaningful human agency;
- responsibility;
- actionable communication;
- a consequential boundary;
- a death warning;
- speech/intention repeatedly corresponding with narrated reality;
- nakedness without shame;
- knowledge asymmetry without demonstrated actionable disadvantage.

Therefore the humans do not encounter two speakers with equivalent disclosed histories.

### God
**creation → provision → beneficence → relationship → communication → demonstrated correspondence**

### Serpent
At entry into Genesis 3:
**no disclosed prior reliability history → no competing provision → no disclosed epistemic provenance**

This asymmetry governs the entire movement.

## 3. The Whole-Movement Architecture

### Genesis 3:1 — Competing representation enters
The serpent foregrounds prohibition and materially distorts the scope of God’s prior provision.

### Genesis 3:2 — Human correction
The woman understands the distortion and corrects it: **“We may eat.”** Her first response is corrective rather than capitulatory.

### Genesis 3:3 — Boundary preserved
The woman demonstrates knowledge of general provision, the specific prohibition, shared human applicability, and a death consequence. “Do not touch” introduces unresolved provenance but does not demonstrate rebellion, attraction, or distrust.

### Genesis 3:4 — Direct contradiction
The serpent escalates from reframing to emphatic contradiction: **“You shall not surely die.”** It supplies a counterclaim, not counterevidence.

### Genesis 3:5 — Alternative interpretive framework
The serpent claims knowledge of God’s knowledge, predicts opened eyes and knowledge, and presents prospective elevation/gain. Its claims increase in consequence faster than its disclosed evidence.

### Genesis 3:6 — Human evaluation changes
For the first time the prohibited alternative becomes attractive. The woman independently evaluates the tree as good for food, visually desirable, and desirable in relation to the proposed knowledge/wisdom gain. Her agency remains; its direction changes. She eats and gives to her husband; he eats.

### Genesis 3:7 — Resulting lived condition begins
Eyes open and new knowing occurs. An unchanged condition—nakedness—now elicits concealment and self-remediation. Human agency survives. The serpent supplies no narrated assistance. The acquired knowledge is experientially consequential.

### Unit movement
**distortion → correction → boundary preservation → contradiction → reinterpretation/prospective gain → changed evaluation/attraction → action → experiential consequence**

This architecture is a principal synthesis finding.

---

## 4. The Woman’s Trajectory Must Not Be Flattened

The completed movement decisively rejects a retrospective reading in which the woman is already gullible, rebellious, tempted, or cognitively compromised from the beginning.

Through 3:3 she:
- understands the serpent;
- corrects its scope;
- preserves God’s abundant permission;
- knows the boundary;
- knows a serious consequence;
- speaks corporately: **we**.

Through 3:5:
- inducements have appeared;
- attraction has still not been narrated.

Only in 3:6 does attraction become visible.

Therefore:

> **The woman’s movement is not narrated as immediate capitulation. It is a progression from demonstrated boundary-preserving knowledge through sustained competing representation to changed evaluation, attraction, and action.**

**Unit status:** High-confidence literary finding.

This confirms the methodological discipline **Availability ≠ Temptation** and strengthens **Inducement ≠ Temptation**.

---

## 5. The Serpent’s Method Is Progressive Rather Than Merely Contradictory

Across the unit, the serpent does not begin with its strongest claim.

Its disclosed progression is:

**reframe God’s words**  
→ **receive correction**  
→ **directly contradict God’s consequence**  
→ **claim superior/alternative knowledge**  
→ **reinterpret the boundary as connected to blocked gain**  
→ **present prospective epistemic elevation**

The woman is never explicitly commanded to eat.

Therefore:

> **The serpent’s disclosed influence operates principally by reconstructing relationships among God, boundary, consequence, knowledge, and prospective human condition rather than by narrated physical coercion or explicit behavioral command.**

**Unit status:** High-confidence analytical finding.

The movement supports inducing boundary crossing as a purpose of the serpent’s communication, while complete motive remains undisclosed.

---

## 6. Counterclaim Never Becomes Counterevidence Before the Decision

The serpent’s assertions become increasingly consequential, but the narrative supplies no pre-decision:
- demonstration;
- witness;
- prior verified prediction;
- example of safe eating;
- evidence of divine unreliability;
- explanation of epistemic provenance;
- alternative provision.

Meanwhile God’s disclosed history already includes repeated speech/reality correspondence and beneficent provision.

Therefore:

> **The serpent’s increasing certainty and explanatory sophistication do not repair its evidentiary deficit before the humans act.**

And:

> **The human decision occurs without the narrative first supplying counterevidence that overturns God’s accumulated reliability.**

**Unit status:** High-confidence epistemic finding.

This confirms:
- **Counterclaim ≠ counterevidence**
- **Knowledge Asymmetry ≠ Epistemic Authority**
- **Increasing certainty ≠ evidentiary provenance**

## 7. Four-Dimensional Asymmetry

The completed unit strengthens a recurring asymmetry.

### Material
God supplies creation, bodies, garden, food, vegetation, capacities.  
The serpent supplies no alternative material world.

### Relational
God has an accumulated history of provision, attention, communication, blessing, and responsibility.  
The serpent has no disclosed comparable beneficent history.

### Epistemic
God’s speech has accumulated correspondence with narrated reality.  
The serpent claims consequential knowledge without disclosed provenance.

### Evidential
God’s representation is embedded in an existing evidence ecology.  
The serpent’s representation becomes behaviorally influential before it becomes evidentially stronger.

Therefore:

> **The serpent offers an alternative interpretation of God’s reality without supplying an alternative reality, comparable relational history, or disclosed evidentiary basis of its own.**

**Unit status:** Strong synthesis finding.

---

## 8. Replicated Interpretive Action — Whole-Unit Adjudication

Verse 5 generated the hypothesis that influence might operate not merely by transferring conclusions but by inducing another agent to reproduce an interpretive operation.

The completed unit supplies two major tests.

### Serpent
Takes God’s disclosed reality as raw material and rearranges relationships:
**boundary → restriction/blockage**  
**death warning → denied**  
**knowledge asymmetry → prospective advantage**  
**eating → gain**

### Woman — 3:6
The same tree is now independently evaluated:
**good for food → desirable to eyes → desirable for knowledge/wisdom gain**

### Humans — 3:7
The same nakedness that previously coexisted with no shame is now known/experienced as requiring concealment.

Therefore:

> **The interpretive architecture first observed in the serpent’s reframing recurs in human perception: materially unchanged created realities acquire altered relationships of meaning that generate changed action.**

**Unit status:** Strong analytical finding.

The stronger statement:

> **The humans consciously imitate the serpent’s reasoning**

remains unestablished.

The hypothesis that the serpent’s interpretive operation has been **internalized** is strongly strengthened but should remain explanatory rather than textual.

---

## 9. Interpretation Becomes Lived Reality

Across 3:6–7:

### Tree
**unchanged reality → changed evaluation → changed action**

### Nakedness
**unchanged reality → changed knowing/evaluation → changed action**

This replication authorizes:

> **Changed interpretation can materially change human relationship to reality without materially changing the reality itself.**

**Unit status:** High-confidence Nature-of-Reality finding.

The completed movement therefore shows a consequential chain:

**representation → interpretation/belief → perception/evaluation → action → lived experience → further interpretation/action**

This is one of the principal synthesis discoveries.

---

## 10. Human Agency Remains Throughout

The serpent’s influence never requires the disappearance of human agency.

The woman:
- understands;
- retrieves prior information;
- corrects;
- evaluates;
- desires;
- takes;
- eats.

After the crossing, the humans:
- perceive;
- know;
- use resources;
- construct;
- cover.

Therefore:

> **Human agency remains operative before, during, and after the boundary crossing. What changes is how that agency is directed.**

**Unit status:** High-confidence finding.

This confirms **Agency Before Misuse** and **Consequential Creaturely Agency**. Misuse does not retroactively define agency itself.

---

## 11. Knowledge: From Prospective Gain to Lived Consequence

The serpent presents:
**eyes opened → knowledge**

After eating:
**eyes opened → they knew**

The correspondence is real.

But Genesis does not define the acquired knowledge abstractly. It shows what possessing it does to lived experience.

The humans already were naked. After the crossing, their new knowing changes their relationship to that existing condition and produces concealment.

Therefore:

> **The acquired knowledge is demonstrably experientially consequential.**

**Unit status:** High-confidence finding.

This does not exhaustively define “knowledge of good and evil” as experiential knowledge of evil.

The narrower synthesis survives:

> **Genesis 3:5–7 makes knowledge visible through changed lived relationship and behavior.**

---

## 12. Outcome Prediction Versus Outcome Characterization

The serpent predicts:
- opened eyes;
- new knowing.

Both correspond with verse 7.

Therefore simplistic claims that “the serpent lied about everything” are not authorized.

But correspondence of individual propositions does not settle the complete representation.

The prospective framing presents the changed state as desirable gain. The first narrated lived manifestation includes an unchanged created condition becoming something the humans seek to conceal.

Therefore:

> **Outcome Prediction ≠ Outcome Characterization.**

And:

> **Propositional Correspondence ≠ Representational Faithfulness.**

**Unit status:** Strongly confirmed representational distinctions.

The correct unit-level question is not merely:

> **Was the serpent technically right?**

but:

> **Did the serpent’s complete representation faithfully orient the humans toward the consequential reality they actually entered?**

Within 3:1–7, that question has begun to receive evidence but is not exhaustively closed.

## 13. Positive Priority Survives the Disruption

Genesis 2 supplied:
- beneficence before distrust;
- agency before misuse;
- nakedness before shame/problematic concealment;
- provision before the competing representation of restriction.

Genesis 3:1–7 does not erase those baselines.

Therefore:

> **Later disruption must not become the original definition of what preceded it.**

In particular:
- nakedness is not inherently shameful merely because it becomes problematic after eating;
- agency is not inherently rebellious merely because it becomes directed across the boundary;
- God’s provision is not retroactively scarcity because the serpent reframes the boundary as restriction.

**Unit status:** Positive Priority strongly confirmed.

---

## 14. God’s Boundary: Effect and Purpose Must Remain Distinct

By 3:7, a new experiential condition appears only after the humans cross the boundary.

Therefore:

> **The boundary stood between the humans and the experiential condition entered through eating.**

**Status:** High-confidence effect finding.

This materially strengthens the Protective-Boundary hypothesis.

But:

> **God established the boundary specifically or solely to protect the humans from this condition**

remains a purpose inference.

**Effect ≠ Purpose** remains controlling.

---

## 15. God’s Silence Does Not Mean Prior Absence or Lack of Care

No new divine intervention is narrated while the serpent/human conversation develops, while the woman evaluates the tree, while both humans eat, or while they construct coverings.

But the movement begins after an extensive divine history of:
**creation → provision → blessing → relationship → instruction → boundary → consequence**

Therefore:

> **Absence of new intervention during the decision ≠ prior absence from the relationship.**

And:

> **Divine non-intervention ≠ demonstrated indifference, approval, abandonment, or complete free-will purpose.**

The claim that God only becomes involved after crisis is contradicted by the accumulated revealed history preceding Genesis 3.

## 16. God’s Character — Unit-Level Portrait

Genesis 3:1–7 contains no new divine speech after the serpent enters, but the unit materially tests prior disclosure.

What survives:
- God’s provision remains materially present;
- God’s prior beneficence is not narratively overturned before the decision;
- God’s actionable boundary remains clear enough for the woman to articulate;
- God’s warning is contradicted but not counterevidentially defeated before the action;
- the first post-crossing experiential condition appears on the far side of the boundary;
- nothing in 3:7 says God newly altered the humans’ bodies or made nakedness defective;
- the humans continue using Creator-provided capacities and material resources.

Therefore:

> **The completed 3:1–7 movement supplies no evidence that the Creator became less beneficent, less reliable, or materially less providing before the humans changed their representation of him and crossed the boundary.**

**Unit status:** Strong Character-of-God synthesis finding.

The Protective-Boundary portrait is strengthened but purpose remains provisional.

---

## 17. Nature of God — Unit-Level Findings

The serpent changes the representation of God.

The humans’ evaluation changes.

Their behavior changes.

Their lived experience changes.

God is not narrated as changing within the bounded unit.

Therefore:

> **Changed human interpretation of God does not itself constitute evidence of change in God.**

And:

> **The Creator remains ontologically prior to and materially sustaining of the reality within which the competing interpretation and its consequences unfold.**

The second formulation is a strong Nature-of-God/Nature-of-Reality synthesis grounded in the unit’s Creator/created asymmetry.

---

## 18. Nature of Reality — Unit-Level Findings

Genesis 3:1–7 strongly supports:

1. **Interpretation can alter lived relationship to materially unchanged reality.**
2. **Information can become behaviorally influential without becoming evidentially stronger.**
3. **Communicative influence and genuine human agency can coexist.**
4. **Agency can remain real while becoming differently directed.**
5. **Knowledge can be experientially consequential.**
6. **More knowledge does not automatically produce a better lived relationship to reality.**
7. **An interpretive architecture can continue shaping human perception after the originating speaker ceases speaking.**
8. **A humanly generated remedy is neither demonstrated adequate nor inadequate merely because it is generated.**
9. **Technical correspondence in communication is not equivalent to representational faithfulness.**
10. **Created resources and capacities remain usable even when humans experience created reality through altered interpretation.**

These are among the strongest unit-level findings.

## 19. The Man: Disclosure Asymmetry Must Remain

The completed unit does not justify importing the woman’s disclosed cognitive pathway into her husband.

For the woman, Genesis supplies:
**conversation → correction → boundary representation → later changed evaluation → attraction → taking → eating**

For the man in verse 6:
**fruit given → eating**

His location during the earlier conversation and at the woman’s eating remains unresolved within the dossiers. His motive remains undisclosed.

Therefore:

> **The complete 3:1–7 synthesis does not authorize reconstruction of the man’s psychology from the woman’s disclosed reasoning.**

This confirms **Disclosure Asymmetry ≠ Ontological Asymmetry** and protects against traditional psychological overreach.

---

## 20. The Serpent’s Absence After the Crossing

Within the bounded unit, the serpent supplies no narrated post-crossing:
- explanation;
- comfort;
- provision;
- clothing;
- guidance;
- companionship;
- remedy.

Therefore:

> **The serpent supplies no narrated assistance within the resulting condition.**

This is a literary finding.

It does not establish malicious abandonment or complete motive.

The more consequential synthesis is:

> **The humans must respond to the new condition using capacities and material resources already belonging to the Creator’s reality.**

## 21. Human Self-Remediation

The first disclosed post-crossing human response is:

**newly perceived problem → humanly generated response → covering**

This uses intelligence, agency, constructive ability, and vegetation that predate the serpent’s intervention.

Therefore:

> **The first human remediation after the crossing is generated from Creator-supplied capacities and resources, not from new serpent-supplied provision.**

**Status:** Strong synthesis finding.

The coverings’ adequacy remains unresolved at the Genesis 3:7 boundary.

## 22. Reader Epistemic Development Across the Unit

The reader’s evidentiary position changes dramatically.

### At 3:1
The reader can compare the serpent’s question with God’s prior words and detect distortion.

### At 3:2–3
The reader sees that the woman possesses substantial prior knowledge and corrects the distortion.

### At 3:4–5
The reader sees direct contradiction and unsupported alternative explanation.

### At 3:6
The reader sees the alternative representation become attractive and behaviorally consequential.

### At 3:7
The reader begins seeing resulting reality.

Therefore the reader is educated from a simple correspondence test toward a richer representational test.

> **Genesis 1 established the importance of speech/reality correspondence; Genesis 3:1–7 shows why isolated correspondence is insufficient when a communicator can frame corresponding propositions within a materially misleading total representation.**

**Status:** Strong wide-angle synthesis hypothesis/finding candidate.

This should remain carefully worded because “why Genesis intended” is purpose language. The observable reader effect is strong; complete communicative purpose remains open.

## 27. Methodological Adjudication from the Completed Unit

The complete 3:1–7 movement now permits adjudication of candidates generated prospectively.

### MA-01 — Inducement ≠ Temptation
**PROMOTE.**

> **Information capable of attracting a person does not establish temptation until attraction is evidenced in the recipient.**

Genesis 3:5 contains inducements; Genesis 3:6 supplies attraction.

### MA-02 — Propositional Correspondence ≠ Representational Faithfulness
**PROMOTE.**

> **A communication may contain propositions that correspond with resulting reality while its total representation still requires examination for framing, omission, provenance, uptake, and lived consequence.**

Verse 7 provides direct testing.

### MA-03 — Technical Accuracy ≠ Truthfulness of the Communicative Act
**PROMOTE WITH REPRESENTATIONAL-FAITHFULNESS CONTROL.**

Technical accuracy is one evidentiary dimension, not the whole communicative judgment.

### MA-04 — Experiential Acquisition Audit
**PROMOTE.**

> **When knowledge is acquired through action, test whether the resulting knowledge is manifested partly through participation in or changed lived relationship to the reality known.**

Do not force “experience” into a lexical definition.

### MA-05 — Replicated Interpretive Action
**PROMOTE WITH NARROWING.**

> **Influence may operate not merely by transferring conclusions but by reproducing an interpretive architecture in which existing realities are reorganized into altered relationships of meaning.**

Do not require conscious imitation unless independently demonstrated.

### MA-06 — Knowledge Asymmetry ≠ Epistemic Authority
**PROMOTE.**

Claiming inaccessible knowledge does not establish reliability.

### MA-07 — Value ≠ Legitimate Means of Acquisition
**PRESERVE/PROMOTE.**

The perceived value of knowledge does not establish the legitimacy or benefit of every means of acquiring it.

### MA-08 — Narrative Sufficiency Before Technical Expansion
**PROMOTE.**

> **Before expanding a local question into specialist complexity, determine whether disclosed narrative relationships already provide sufficient evidence for responsible adjudication.**

Genesis 3:7 prospectively validated this order.

### MA-09 — Technical Complexity ≠ Interpretive Necessity
**PROMOTE.**

A genuine lexical or scholarly complexity does not automatically require resolution for the claim presently being adjudicated.

### MA-10 — Specialist Testing, Not Specialist Preemption
**PROMOTE.**

> **Specialist study should test, refine, challenge, or increase the resolution of first-reader recognition where materially relevant; it should not preempt narratively sufficient recognition merely because greater technical complexity is available.**

Diagnostic:
> **Do not let a harder question replace the question the text has already answered.**

### MA-11 — Influence ≠ Evidentiary Authorization
**PROMOTE.**

Behavioral influence does not establish evidentiary warrant.

### MA-12 — Redirected Agency
**PROMOTE.**

> **Influence may redirect genuine agency without eliminating the agency of the influenced person.**

### MA-13 — Outcome Prediction ≠ Outcome Characterization
**PROMOTE.**

Accurate prediction that change will occur does not establish faithful characterization of the changed condition.

### MA-14 — Experienced Meaning Can Change Without Material Change
**PROMOTE.**

A materially unchanged reality may acquire radically different experienced meaning when interpretive relationship changes.

### MA-15 — Observation Before Emotional Explanation
**PROMOTE.**

Secure changed behavior may be recognized while precise emotional explanation remains open.

### MA-16 — Remedy Status Requires Separate Adjudication
**PROMOTE.**

A generated remedy establishes neither its adequacy nor inadequacy.

## 28. Earlier Methodological Candidate Tested: Prospective Disclosure / Prospective Anchor

The Post–Genesis 2:4–25 methodology held:

> **A disclosure may establish an anchor before later relationships provide the context necessary to appreciate its fuller significance.**

Genesis 3:1–7 supplies a strong prospective test.

Examples:
- **naked/not ashamed** becomes crucial only when nakedness is later known/experienced differently;
- **the exact boundary and consequence** become crucial when another speaker reframes and contradicts them;
- **knowledge of good and evil** becomes crucial when knowledge is presented as prospective gain and then becomes lived;
- **abundant provision** becomes crucial when the serpent foregrounds restriction.

Therefore:

> **Earlier disclosure can establish a stable evidentiary anchor whose fuller significance becomes visible only through later relationships, without allowing later material to rewrite the earlier state.**

**Synthesis recommendation:** **PROMOTE Prospective Disclosure / Prospective Anchor**, with the safeguard that later significance may deepen an earlier anchor but may not manufacture wording, psychology, or purpose absent from the earlier disclosure.

## 31. Synthesis Conclusion

> **Genesis 3:1–7 presents a movement from competing representation to lived consequence. The serpent first reframes God’s abundant provision around prohibition. The woman initially resists that framing: she understands it, corrects it, and preserves the shared provision and consequential boundary she knows. The narrative therefore does not begin with a gullible or already rebellious woman, nor does temptation arise merely because a prohibited alternative exists.**
>
> **The serpent escalates. Reframing becomes direct contradiction; contradiction becomes an alternative explanation of God, boundary, knowledge, consequence, and prospective human condition. These increasingly consequential claims are not accompanied by corresponding increases in disclosed evidence, epistemic provenance, provision, or reliability history. Yet information need not become evidentially stronger to become behaviorally influential.**
>
> **In Genesis 3:6, the prohibited alternative finally becomes attractive. The woman now evaluates the unchanged tree within relationships compatible with the alternative framework: good for food, desirable to the eyes, desirable in relation to the proposed knowledge/wisdom gain. Human agency has not disappeared. It remains genuine while becoming differently directed. The serpent need not physically coerce or explicitly command the boundary crossing.**
>
> **Genesis 3:7 then begins exposing lived consequence. The serpent’s predictions of opened eyes and new knowing receive genuine propositional correspondence. But the resulting knowledge is not narratively inert or merely abstract. The humans now experience an already-existing condition—nakedness—in a way that immediately changes their behavior toward it. The same architecture visible with the tree therefore appears again: materially unchanged reality acquires altered experienced meaning and produces changed action.**
>
> **This recurring architecture strongly supports Replicated Interpretive Action at the structural level. The serpent takes God’s disclosed reality and rearranges its relationships into an alternative account; subsequently, human perception itself begins operating through altered relationships of meaning. Conscious imitation is not demonstrated, and internalization remains an explanatory hypothesis, but the structural recurrence is strong.**
>
> **Human agency survives the entire movement. Before the crossing it can correct distortion; during the crossing it can be redirected by changed evaluation; after the crossing it becomes active in self-remediation. The humans use capacities and resources belonging to the Creator’s already-existing provision. The serpent supplies an alternative interpretation of God’s reality without supplying an alternative reality in which to live.**
>
> **The unit also changes how communicative truthfulness must be evaluated. The serpent is not simply wrong about every proposition: eyes open and new knowing occurs. Yet accurate outcome prediction is not identical to faithful outcome characterization. Propositional correspondence is therefore insufficient by itself to adjudicate representational faithfulness. The reader must compare framing, omission, provenance, uptake, and lived consequence with the reality actually entered.**
>
> **For the Character-of-God investigation, the most important restraint is equally consequential. The humans’ changed perception does not constitute evidence that God’s previously disclosed provision was defective. God’s beneficence, provision, actionable clarity, and accumulated reliability are not narratively overturned before the decision. The boundary demonstrably stood between the humans and the experiential condition entered through crossing it; a protective purpose is strongly strengthened but remains distinct from the observed effect.**
>
> **At the Genesis 3:7 boundary, the reader therefore possesses a much richer evidentiary position than the humans possessed when the competing representation first appeared. The movement has shown how representation becomes perception, how perception directs agency, how action becomes lived experience, and how lived experience can expose dimensions of a representation that isolated propositions cannot. The portrait remains open to later correction, but nothing beyond Genesis 3:7 is required or permitted for the judgments frozen here.**
