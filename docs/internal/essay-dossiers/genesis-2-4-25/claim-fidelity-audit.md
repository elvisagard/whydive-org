# Three sentences worth tightening

These are the only places where I would modify the authoritative draft.

### 1. “The One who supplied food”

Current:

> “This is the One who gave the human life.
>
> The One who established his place.
>
> The One who supplied food.”

This is authorized, but the **human's personal evidentiary access** to all the narrated history is not always identical to the **reader's evidentiary access**.

Our dossier explicitly distinguished those positions.

Better:

> **The reader already knows this as the One who gave the human life, established his place, and supplied his food.**

Then we can say that the human himself has directly received the resulting life, place, provision, and command.

That preserves reader/character evidence precisely.

---

### 2. “The Lord can establish a world in which a creature's decisions genuinely matter.”

This is supported.

But “a world in which” could sound universal from one naming episode.

Better:

> **The Lord can establish situations in which a creature's decisions genuinely matter.**

That matches our adjudicated **Consequential Creaturely Agency** formulation more exactly.

---

### 3. “The wider created reality contains forms of value…”

Current:

> “the wider created reality contains forms of value whose complete significance has not yet been explained.”

“Value” is defensible because the gold is called good and the trees desirable, but the sentence merges the garden vegetation and wider-world materials slightly.

Better:

> **the wider created reality contains notable features whose complete significance has not yet been explained.**

Then keep **multidimensional value** specifically attached to the trees, where the textual warrant is strongest.

Those are **fidelity refinements**, not substantive corrections.
