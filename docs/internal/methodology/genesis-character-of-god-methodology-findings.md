# Genesis Character-of-God Methodology Findings

## Governed Addendum Registry

This document preserves methodological findings adopted after GRA-01 v1.0 without modifying the controlling protocol inside an individual essay implementation.

GRA-01 remains the controlling retrospective-audit protocol. Findings recorded here govern subsequent Genesis Character-of-God investigations unless later evidence revises them through explicit methodology governance.

## MR-168-01 — Revelatory-Premise Safeguard

**Status:** ADOPTED

The existing safeguard remains:

> **Effect is not purpose.**

Within a Character-of-God investigation that receives Scripture as divine-human revelation, responsibly established features of the revelation may be treated as intentionally available to the reader within God's revelatory act. Intentional availability does not by itself establish God's particular purpose for choosing that feature or form.

Preserve the sequence:

> **textual feature → demonstrable reader effect → intentional revelatory availability → specific-purpose hypothesis**

External thinkers may challenge textual observation, translation, source-language analysis, literary relationship, inference, reader effect, or a claimed particular purpose without sharing the study's theology of revelation.

This safeguard must not be used to dismiss legitimate textual or historical criticism because a source works from different theological premises.

## MR-168-03 — Reflective Return Principle

**Status:** ADOPTED

The Relational/Reader Lens includes this question:

> **How does accumulated encounter change what the reader is now able to notice, question, compare, remember, and recognize?**

A later encounter may generate a question that sends the reader back to earlier evidence.

> **The later encounter may generate the question; the earlier evidence must still authorize the answer.**

Permanent safeguards:

- **New salience is not new evidence.**
- **Reflective return is not backward importation.**
- **Reader engagement generates candidate recognition; evidence adjudicates it.**

Backward importation allows a later conclusion to control what an earlier passage is permitted to mean. Reflective Return uses the later encounter to generate a question, then returns to the earlier evidence so that the earlier evidence can independently support, weaken, or reject the candidate recognition.

## MR-168-02 — Early Evidence Ecology

**Status:** CARRY FORWARD FOR TESTING — NOT ADOPTED AS SETTLED METHODOLOGY

Preserve this candidate sequence for prospective testing:

> **revelatory testimony → exposed correspondence within testimony → repetition/accumulated history → increasing referential access → experiential correspondence**

Genesis 1:6–8 primarily supplies the first three stages. Genesis 1:9–13 adds evidence relevant to increasing referential and experiential access.

Genesis 1:14 onward retains full authority to strengthen, complicate, reorder, expand, or break the candidate sequence. It must not be represented as a constitutional feature of the method unless later testing warrants adoption.
