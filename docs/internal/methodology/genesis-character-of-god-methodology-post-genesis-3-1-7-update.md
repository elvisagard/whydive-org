## 2. First-Reader Priority — Explicitly Reaffirmed

The Genesis Character-of-God study began from the standpoint of an attentive non-specialist reader.

Genesis 3:6 exposed a methodological danger: as the method becomes more sophisticated, technical questions can begin displacing relationships already visible in the narrative.

The woman’s perception of the tree as desirable for wisdom followed immediately after the serpent presented knowledge of good and evil as prospective gain. The narrative relationship was available before specialist expansion.

### Principle

> **First-Reader Priority:** Begin by asking what an attentive non-specialist reader can responsibly recognize from the disclosed narrative relationships before introducing specialist resolution.

This does not privilege naïveté over scholarship.

The first reader may be wrong.

Source-language evidence, translation comparison, historical context, and external scholarship may correct, refine, or overturn a first-reader recognition.

But specialist competence is not the prerequisite for responsible attention.

### Governing order

**first-reader encounter**  
→ **narrative relationships**  
→ **provisional judgment**  
→ **identify material gap**  
→ **smallest necessary specialist test**  
→ **return findings to narrative**  
→ **revise if materially required**

---

## 3. Narrative Sufficiency Before Technical Expansion — PROMOTED

### Principle

> **Narrative Sufficiency Before Technical Expansion:** Before expanding a local question into lexical, historical, scholarly, or theological complexity, determine whether the disclosed narrative relationships already provide sufficient evidence for the judgment actually required.

### Diagnostic question

> **Does the text already give us enough to answer the question we are actually asking?**

If yes, specialist inquiry may still test the judgment, but it should not manufacture a larger problem merely because a harder technical question exists.

### Safeguard

> **Do not let a harder question replace the question the text has already answered.**

Genesis 3:7 prospectively validated this principle. The first reader could recognize:

**“you will know” → eating → “they knew.”**

A bounded technical pass then tested rather than generated that recognition.

---

## 4. Technical Complexity ≠ Interpretive Necessity — PROMOTED

### Principle

> **Technical Complexity ≠ Interpretive Necessity:** The existence of genuine lexical, textual, historical, translation, or scholarly complexity does not establish that resolving that complexity is necessary for the claim presently being adjudicated.

A technical question becomes methodologically necessary when its resolution could materially alter a consequential judgment.

This principle extends **Interesting Question ≠ Research Obligation** into specialist interpretation.

---

## 5. Specialist Testing, Not Specialist Preemption — PROMOTED

### Principle

> **Specialist Testing, Not Specialist Preemption:** Source-language study, translation comparison, historical context, scholarship, and external dialogue should test, refine, challenge, or increase the resolution of first-reader recognition when materially relevant; they should not preempt narratively sufficient recognition merely because greater technical complexity is available.

### Safeguard

Scholarship may:
- expose a translation artifact;
- identify ambiguity;
- challenge an assumed relationship;
- add relevant historical constraints;
- reveal counterevidence;
- increase local resolution.

Scholarship should not:
- become the permission structure that determines whether a non-specialist reader is allowed to notice a narrative relationship;
- convert every lexical range into a research obligation;
- create artificial uncertainty around claims whose necessary narrative evidence is already sufficient.

## 6. Inducement ≠ Temptation — PROMOTED

The existing method already established:

> **Availability ≠ Temptation.**

Genesis 3:1–6 adds a needed refinement.

The serpent can supply:
- information;
- alternatives;
- questions;
- proposed benefits;
- prospective elevation;
- inducements;

without the narrative yet demonstrating attractive pull in the human recipient.

Through Genesis 3:5, inducements exist but human attraction is not narrated.

Genesis 3:6 supplies the first disclosed attraction.

### Principle

> **Inducement ≠ Temptation:** Information or prospective benefit capable of attracting a person does not establish temptation in that person until attraction, enticement, desire, or comparable pull is evidenced in the recipient.

### Relationship to prior principle

**Availability ≠ Temptation** protects against equating the existence of an option with temptation.

**Inducement ≠ Temptation** protects against equating an offered attraction with demonstrated attraction in the recipient.

Both remain.

---

## 7. Influence ≠ Evidentiary Authorization — PROMOTED

Genesis 3:1–6 demonstrates that a representation may become behaviorally consequential without acquiring stronger disclosed evidence.

### Principle

> **Influence ≠ Evidentiary Authorization:** The fact that information persuades, attracts, redirects behavior, or becomes culturally/psychologically influential does not establish that the information has acquired evidentiary warrant.

Ask separately:

1. Did the representation influence the recipient?
2. What evidence authorized belief in the representation?

Do not infer the second from the first.

---

## 8. Knowledge Asymmetry ≠ Epistemic Authority — PROMOTED

The prior method established:

> **Knowledge Asymmetry Without Actionable Disadvantage.**

Genesis 3 adds another side of the problem.

The serpent claims access to consequential knowledge unavailable to the humans.

But claimed informational advantage does not itself establish trustworthiness.

### Principle

> **Knowledge Asymmetry ≠ Epistemic Authority:** Possessing or claiming information another person lacks does not by itself establish reliability, authority, or evidentiary superiority.

### Safeguard

> **Claimed access ≠ verified provenance.**

This should trigger an **Epistemic Provenance Audit** when consequential claims depend upon a speaker’s asserted informational advantage.


## 9. Epistemic Provenance Audit — PROMOTED AS WORKFLOW CONTROL

Genesis 3:4–5 repeatedly raises the question:

> **How does this speaker know what this speaker claims to know?**

### Workflow control

For consequential claims, ask:

- What is being claimed?
- How certain is the claim presented?
- What source of knowledge is disclosed?
- What evidence is supplied?
- What prior reliability history is available?
- Is informational asymmetry being used rhetorically as though it were authority?

### Safeguard

> **Increasing certainty of assertion does not repair absent evidentiary provenance.**

This is a workflow control rather than a claim that every narrative must disclose a speaker’s complete epistemic history.

---

## 10. Replicated Interpretive Action — PROMOTED WITH NARROWING

Genesis 3:5 generated the hypothesis that influence may operate not merely by transferring conclusions but by reproducing an interpretive operation.

The completed 3:1–7 movement supplied structural replication:

### Serpent
Existing disclosed realities are rearranged:
**boundary → blocked gain**  
**warning → denied consequence**  
**knowledge asymmetry → prospective advantage**

### Woman
The materially unchanged tree is independently reevaluated as:
**good for food → desirable → desirable for knowledge/wisdom gain**

### Humans
Previously non-problematic nakedness becomes a condition requiring concealment.

### Principle

> **Replicated Interpretive Action:** Influence may operate not merely by transferring conclusions but by reproducing an interpretive architecture in which existing realities are reorganized into altered relationships of meaning that generate new evaluations and actions.

### Critical narrowing

Structural recurrence does **not** establish:
- conscious imitation;
- complete internalization;
- identical motives;
- identical propositions;
- loss of agency.

Therefore:

> **Structural replication ≠ demonstrated conscious imitation.**

---

## 11. Redirected Agency — PROMOTED

The prior methodology established genuine and consequential human agency before misuse.

Genesis 3 demonstrates that influence and agency need not be mutually exclusive.

### Principle

> **Redirected Agency:** Influence may alter the direction, object, or purpose of genuine creaturely agency without eliminating the agency of the influenced person.

### Safeguards

> **Influence ≠ replacement of agency.**

> **Misguided action ≠ absence of agency.**

This principle remains bounded from later philosophical debates concerning determinism, libertarian freedom, or exhaustive models of volition.

---

## 12. Experiential Acquisition Audit — PROMOTED

Genesis 3:5–7 links:
**prospective knowledge → action → opened eyes → knowing → changed lived relationship**

The method should not force the verb “know” to mean “experience” lexically.

Instead, the narrative sequence may demonstrate experiential consequentiality.

### Principle

> **Experiential Acquisition Audit:** When knowledge is acquired through action, test whether the resulting knowledge becomes manifest partly through participation in, changed relationship to, or lived experience of the reality known.

### Questions

- Was the object/reality already factually available?
- What changed after the acquisition?
- Did perception, evaluation, relationship, or behavior change?
- Is the experiential conclusion derived from narrative sequence or being forced from a lexical definition?

### Safeguard

> **Experiential manifestation ≠ exhaustive lexical definition.**

---

## 13. Experienced Meaning Can Change Without Material Change — PROMOTED

Genesis 3:6–7 supplies two replications:

**same tree → changed evaluation/action**

**same nakedness → changed knowing/action**

### Principle

> **Experienced Meaning Can Change Without Material Change:** A materially unchanged reality may acquire substantially different experienced meaning when the knower’s interpretive relationship to that reality changes.

This is primarily a Nature-of-Reality finding, but it also functions methodologically by preventing interpreters from inventing material changes when changed perception already has adequate narrative evidence.

### Safeguard

> **Changed experience ≠ demonstrated changed object.**

---

## 14. Propositional Correspondence ≠ Representational Faithfulness — PROMOTED

Genesis 3:7 confirms that the serpent’s predictions of opened eyes and new knowing correspond with resulting reality.

That correspondence must not be weakened.

But individual proposition-level correspondence cannot by itself adjudicate the complete communicative representation.

### Principle

> **Propositional Correspondence ≠ Representational Faithfulness:** A communication may contain propositions that correspond with reality while the complete representation still requires examination of framing, relationships among propositions, consequential omissions, epistemic provenance, means of acquisition, recipient uptake, and resulting lived reality.

### Representational Faithfulness Audit

Ask:

1. Which propositions correspond?
2. Which do not?
3. How were corresponding propositions framed?
4. What did the representation invite the recipient to anticipate?
5. Were consequential relationships omitted?
6. What epistemic provenance was supplied?
7. What means of acquisition were proposed?
8. How did the recipient interpret and act?
9. How does resulting reality compare with the representation as a whole?

### Safeguard

> **Do not protect a preferred speaker by denying genuine propositional correspondence.**

Truthful evaluation requires conceding correspondence wherever it exists.

---

## 15. Technical Accuracy ≠ Truthfulness of the Communicative Act — MERGED

This Genesis 3 candidate is retained but merged under **Representational Faithfulness** rather than maintained as an independent major principle.

Technical/propositional accuracy is one evidentiary dimension.

The complete communicative act may also involve:
- framing;
- selective emphasis;
- consequential omission;
- unsupported authority;
- means of acquisition;
- anticipated versus lived significance.

### Governing formulation

> **Technical accuracy contributes to, but does not exhaust, evaluation of communicative faithfulness.**

---

## 16. Outcome Prediction ≠ Outcome Characterization — PROMOTED

Genesis 3:5 predicts a changed condition.

Genesis 3:7 supplies the changed condition.

### Principle

> **Outcome Prediction ≠ Outcome Characterization:** Accurately predicting that a change will occur does not establish that the significance, quality, cost, or lived character of the changed condition has been faithfully represented.

This principle should be used when a speaker correctly predicts an event but also frames the event prospectively.

### Safeguard

Do not infer deception merely because every consequence was not disclosed.

Ask whether omitted or framed dimensions materially affected what the representation invited the hearer to anticipate.

---

## 17. Observation Before Emotional Explanation — PROMOTED

Genesis 3:7 securely shows changed behavior toward nakedness.

The exact emotional diagnosis is less secure.

### Principle

> **Observation Before Emotional Explanation:** When behavior securely demonstrates a changed relationship to a condition, preserve that observation even when the precise emotional or psychological explanation remains open.

Applied example:

**secure:** nakedness now elicits concealment.

**strong inference:** shame is relevant because 2:25 supplied “not ashamed.”

**less secure:** exhaustive emotional explanation.

### Safeguard

> **Behavioral consequence may be clearer than psychological content.**

---

## 18. Remedy Status Requires Separate Adjudication — PROMOTED

Genesis 3:7 establishes that the humans make coverings.

It does not establish their adequacy.

### Principle

> **Remedy Status Requires Separate Adjudication:** The existence of a response or remedy establishes neither its adequacy nor its inadequacy unless the relevant evidence evaluates the result.

### Safeguards

> **Attempted remedy ≠ successful remedy.**

> **Attempted remedy ≠ failed remedy.**

This protects the information horizon from later evidence arriving prematurely.


## 19. Prospective Disclosure / Prospective Anchor — PROMOTED FROM HOLD

The Post–Genesis 2:4–25 update held this candidate for later testing.

Genesis 3:1–7 supplies strong prospective validation.

Earlier disclosures become consequential anchors:
- **naked / not ashamed** becomes essential for interpreting 3:7;
- **abundant permission** becomes essential for evaluating the serpent’s restriction-framing;
- **the exact boundary and consequence** become essential when contradicted;
- **knowledge of good and evil** becomes essential when presented as prospective gain and then acquired.

### Principle

> **Prospective Disclosure / Prospective Anchor:** A disclosure may establish a stable evidentiary anchor before later relationships provide the context necessary to appreciate its fuller significance.

### Safeguard

> **Later significance may deepen an earlier disclosure; it may not rewrite the earlier evidentiary state.**

Do not use later evidence to manufacture:
- earlier psychology;
- earlier attraction;
- earlier motive;
- wording not present;
- purpose not yet disclosed.

This principle works together with the frozen information-horizon discipline.

## 20. Positive Priority — STRONGLY CONFIRMED

Genesis 3:1–7 prospectively confirms the importance of preserving positive categories established before disruption.

Carry forward explicitly:

> **Provision before restriction-framing.**

> **Agency before misuse.**

> **Nakedness before shame/problematic concealment.**

> **Beneficence before distrust.**

### Safeguard

> **Later disruption must not become the original definition of what preceded it.**

This is especially important in passages later associated with sin, judgment, shame, conflict, or death.

## 21. External Dialogue — Refinement

Genesis 3:7 supplied competing interpretations such as:
- maturation/culture;
- sexual awakening;
- vulnerability;
- shame;
- moral/divine wisdom;
- serpent vindication.

Their disagreement clarified an important methodological relationship.

### Principle refinement

> **External disagreement may strengthen confidence in a shared observation while weakening confidence in any one explanation.**

Ask:

1. What textual anomaly or relationship do multiple interpreters independently notice?
2. Where do their explanations diverge?
3. What observation survives without adopting their explanatory system?

### Safeguard

> **Recognition of a problem ≠ authorization of a commentator’s solution.**

## 22. Local Confidence — Strengthened Again

Genesis 3 repeatedly contains strong findings beside local uncertainty.

Examples:
- “do not touch” provenance unresolved while the woman’s knowledge of provision/boundary remains strong;
- *elohim* referential complexity local while prospective knowledge-associated elevation remains;
- exact emotion behind covering uncertain while changed relationship to nakedness is clear;
- protective purpose uncertain while boundary effect is strong;
- man’s motive unknown while his eating is certain.

### Strengthened principle

> **Local uncertainty must remain attached to the claim whose evidence warrants it; it must not be allowed to dissolve neighboring findings whose evidence is stronger.**

## 23. Psychological-Projection Control — Strengthened

Genesis 3 generated repeated temptations to insert familiar later-human psychology into earlier narrative states.

Rejected projections included:
- woman already resents God;
- woman is gullible;
- woman is intellectually weaker;
- man is silently complicit throughout;
- man eats from romantic solidarity;
- serpent absence means malicious abandonment.

### Principle refinement

> **Later-human plausibility ≠ local narrative evidence.**

Human experience may generate hypotheses and correspondence checks.

It may not establish an undisclosed psychological state.

---

## 24. Disclosure Asymmetry — Strengthened for Multi-Agent Narratives

The woman receives detailed cognitive disclosure.

The man’s eating receives very little.

### Principle refinement

> **Do not transfer one agent’s disclosed cognitive pathway to another agent merely because both eventually perform the same action.**

This extends **Disclosure Asymmetry ≠ Ontological Asymmetry** into motive and psychology.

### Safeguard

> **Same action ≠ demonstrated same reasoning.**

## 25. Representational Competition Audit — NEW WORKFLOW CONTROL

Genesis 3:1–7 introduces sustained competition between representations of the same reality.

When two speakers characterize the same reality differently, add this audit.

### Step 1 — Establish prior evidence
What history does the recipient already possess concerning each speaker?

### Step 2 — Compare representations
What does each speaker say about:
- reality;
- consequence;
- motive;
- value;
- prospective outcome?

### Step 3 — Track evidence
What evidence or provenance accompanies each representation?

### Step 4 — Track recipient uptake
When does the recipient:
- correct;
- question;
- preserve;
- reinterpret;
- become attracted;
- act?

### Step 5 — Compare resulting reality
When consequences become available, compare them with the complete representation rather than isolated propositions only.

### Step 6 — Preserve asymmetry
Do not manufacture evidentiary parity merely because two voices exist.

---

## 26. Lived-Experience Audit — NEW WORKFLOW CONTROL

Because Genesis repeatedly surrounds knowledge and belief with lived consequence, add the following when relevant.

Ask:

- What was the lived condition before the belief/knowledge/action?
- What remains materially continuous?
- What changes in perception, evaluation, relationship, or behavior?
- Does the narrative define the concept abstractly, or show it through lived consequence?
- Are later human experiences being projected backward?
- Does the new experience reveal an effect without yet revealing purpose?

### Safeguard

> **Conceptual language should be tested against what the narrative shows people actually experiencing and doing.**

This audit does not reduce theology to psychology. It protects the narrative’s own relational and experiential evidence.

Not every Genesis 3 discovery should become a methodological rule.

The following remain primarily passage-level portrait or Nature-of-Reality findings:

### Serpent supplies interpretation but no alternative created reality
Important Genesis 3 synthesis; not a universal method rule.

### First post-crossing response is self-remediation
Important local finding; not a general principle that all post-boundary human behavior is self-remediation.

### No narrated serpent assistance after the crossing
Local literary finding; not a universal statement about deceptive agents.

### Boundary stood between humans and entered condition
Character/Nature-of-Reality finding; **Effect ≠ Purpose** already supplies the methodological control.

### More knowledge does not necessarily improve lived condition
Strong Nature-of-Reality finding; not yet necessary as a separate methodological rule.

## 29. Updated Workflow After Genesis 3:1–7

### Stage 1 — First-reader encounter
Read within the current information horizon.

Record:
- salience;
- sequence;
- contrast;
- repetition;
- changed relationships;
- reader questions.

Do not begin with specialist problems.

### Stage 2 — Narrative sufficiency check
Ask:

> **What does the narrative already authorize without technical expansion?**

### Stage 3 — Microscope
Examine wording, grammar, translation, structure, and source language only where materially consequential.

### Stage 4 — Prospective-anchor check
Identify earlier disclosures whose significance may now have increased.

Do not rewrite their earlier evidentiary state.

### Stage 5 — Wide-Angle Lens
Compare with accumulated revealed history.

Preserve Positive Priority.

### Stage 6 — Relational/Reader Lens
Ask how the disclosure changes:
- reader understanding;
- human relationship;
- perception of God;
- perception of reality.

### Stage 7 — Character / Nature / Reality classification
Separate:
- Character of God;
- Nature of God;
- Nature of Reality.

### Stage 8 — Representational Competition Audit, when applicable
Compare speakers, histories, evidence, framing, uptake, and resulting reality.

### Stage 9 — Experiential Acquisition / Lived-Experience Audit, when applicable
Track how knowledge/belief becomes visible in lived relationship and behavior.

### Stage 10 — External dialogue
Use other minds to:
- expose alternatives;
- identify overlooked relationships;
- challenge confidence;
- distinguish shared observation from disputed explanation.

### Stage 11 — Counterargument pass
Attack the strongest claims.

### Stage 12 — Psychological-projection audit
Remove undisclosed motives/emotions/mental states.

### Stage 13 — Claim-distance and Local Confidence
Keep uncertainty local.

### Stage 14 — Effect/Purpose audit
Do not promote effect into purpose.

### Stage 15 — Remedy-status audit, when applicable
Do not infer success/failure merely from attempted remediation.

### Stage 16 — Formal claim adjudication
Classify claims and preserve negative findings.

### Stage 17 — Revelatory anchors and open questions
Freeze what survives before widening the horizon.

### Stage 18 — Methodological reconciliation after completed movement
Promote only principles prospectively tested or sufficiently supported.
