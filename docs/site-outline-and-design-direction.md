# WhyDive.org Site Outline and Design Direction

## Design Thesis

WhyDive.org should feel like a contemplative framework institute.

It should share the public-mission seriousness and movement quality of chart-ed.cc, but with a quieter, deeper, more philosophical tone. The site should make room for long-form writing and slow thinking. It should feel authoritative without becoming corporate, academic without becoming inaccessible, and visionary without becoming vague.

The writing is the center of gravity. Design should create trust, orientation, and atmosphere around it.

The core content foundation is captured in [WhyDive Foundational Thesis](./whydive-foundational-thesis.md). The site should repeatedly return to its central movement: information becomes conclusions, conclusions become judgments, judgments become decisions, decisions become actions, and actions become consequences.

Implementation should follow [WhyDive.org Implementation Phases](./implementation-phases.md). The design direction in this document defines the target; the implementation phases define the order of work.

## Desired Feeling

- Contemplative
- Authoritative
- Humane
- Spacious
- Philosophical
- Evidence-bound
- Movement-oriented, but not campaign-like
- Institutional, but not bureaucratic

## Design Choices To Preserve Authority

### Typography

Use a serious editorial hierarchy.

- Strong, readable sans-serif for navigation, metadata, labels, and interface text
- Literary serif or high-character display treatment for major headings, pull quotes, and essay moments
- Wide measure limits for landing-page copy, narrower measure limits for essays
- Publication-style metadata on essays and whitepapers

Recommended font system:

- Fraunces for framework voice, major headlines, hero statements, and pull quote moments
- EB Garamond for contemplative long-form writing and essays
- Inter for navigation, metadata, labels, buttons, captions, cards, tables, and structural interface text

The current Montserrat, Cormorant, and Mulish stack came from the starter codebase and should be replaced as part of the brand and visual foundation phase.

### Color

Use a restrained palette.

Recommended direction:

- Warm off-white or parchment background
- Deep ink or blue-black text
- Slate and warm gray support tones
- Muted teal, deep green, or old-gold accent
- Avoid bright education colors, candy gradients, and product-SaaS blues

The site should not feel beige-only, dark-blue-only, or overly polished in a startup way.

### Imagery

Use a few strong images, not decorative image clutter.

Preferred symbolic fields:

- Depth
- Descent
- Evidence
- Thresholds
- Light moving through water
- Archives and marginalia
- Observation
- Reflection
- Maps, constellations, and branching frameworks
- Human scale without stock-photo obviousness

Avoid:

- Generic classroom images
- Generic business teams
- Overly literal AI imagery
- Product dashboard mockups
- Decorative blobs or abstract SaaS gradients

### Layout

The layout should be editorial and architectural.

- Large first viewport with one strong proposition
- Few CTAs, written as invitations to read, explore, study, or trace
- Full-width sections with restrained inner widths
- No nested cards
- Cards only for repeated indexes, essay lists, whitepaper lists, and application summaries
- Essay pages should be first-class, not treated as ordinary blog posts

### Navigation

Initial navigation:

- Foundations
- Essays
- Whitepapers
- Research
- Applications
- Ecosystem

Footer should include Living Spiral Studio LLC legal/contact identity until WhyDive becomes a separate legal entity.

## Launch Site Map

### 1. Home

Purpose:

Establish WhyDive as the parent framework and invite visitors into the central question.

Primary message:

WhyDive helps people align conclusions with evidence so judgment can become more disciplined, humble, and wise.

Foundational statement:

WhyDive is a framework for understanding and improving how human beings move from evidence to action.

Core sections:

- Hero: "Dive into the why behind what you believe, conclude, and decide."
- Governing principle: "Strong conclusions require strong evidence."
- Why this matters: judgments shape decisions, actions, lives, institutions, and societies
- The human problem: modern systems help people acquire information, but rarely help them understand how information becomes judgment
- Framework preview: evidence, reasoning, judgment, action
- Current applications: WhyDive Education, DLS, Chart-Ed Institute, Chart-Ed
- Featured essay: "Strong Conclusions Require Strong Evidence"
- Whitepaper invitation
- Living Spiral Studio LLC legal/contact footer

Primary CTAs:

- Read the framework
- Start with the essay
- Explore applications

### 2. Foundations

Purpose:

Define the framework for serious first-time readers.

Index page sections:

- What WhyDive is
- The framework at a glance
- The developmental model
- The boundary system
- Key concepts
- Recommended reading path

Initial child pages:

- What Is WhyDive?
- WhyDive Framework Overview
- Strong Conclusions Require Strong Evidence
- The Developmental Model
- The Boundary System
- The Violation Taxonomy
- Frequently Asked Questions

### 3. Essays

Purpose:

Create the long-term writing home.

Index categories:

- Judgment
- Evidence
- Decision-Making
- Wisdom
- Human Formation
- AI and Human Reasoning
- Public Discourse
- Ethics and Stewardship
- Human Flourishing

Essay page elements:

- Title
- Deck or abstract
- Author
- Publication date
- Category
- Estimated reading time
- Optional table of contents
- Main essay body
- Pull quotes
- Citations or notes
- Related essays

### 4. Whitepapers

Purpose:

Host formal framework and implementation documents.

Index structure should visibly follow [WhyDive Whitepaper Roadmap](./whydive-whitepaper-roadmap.md):

- Layer 1: Foundational Framework
- Layer 2: Framework Architecture
- Layer 3: Educational Implementation
- Layer 4: Data Literacy Pathway
- Layer 5: Future Domain Implementations

Whitepaper page elements:

- Title
- Abstract
- Version
- Status
- Publication date
- Citation
- Download link when available
- Related essays
- Related applications

Initial whitepapers:

- WhyDive: Strengthening Judgment Under Evidence Constraints
- WhyDive: Bounded Reasoning Development System
- WhyDive Education
- WhyDive Reading
- WhyDive Charts
- Data Literacy Standards
- Chart-Ed Framework

Future whitepapers:

- WhyDive Arguments
- WhyDive Business
- WhyDive Policy
- WhyDive Higher Education
- WhyDive AI Evaluation

Anti-drift rule:

Before writing any future document, answer which entity is speaking, which audience is being addressed, what question is being answered, and what layer of the hierarchy the document belongs to.

### 5. Research Library

Purpose:

Connect WhyDive to scholarship without making the site feel like a static bibliography.

Initial sections:

- Epistemic cognition
- Reflective judgment
- Reasoning research
- Cognitive science
- Decision science
- Philosophy of knowledge
- Wisdom literature
- Learning sciences
- AI and synthetic information

Content formats:

- Annotated bibliographies
- Reading guides
- Research summaries
- Recommended books
- Concept notes

### 6. Applications

Purpose:

Show how WhyDive appears in specific domains while keeping the parent framework distinct from its applications.

Current application pages:

- WhyDive Education
- WhyDive Reading
- WhyDive Charts
- Data Literacy Standards
- Chart-Ed Institute
- Chart-Ed

Future application pages:

- WhyDive Arguments
- WhyDive Business
- WhyDive Policy
- WhyDive Higher Education
- WhyDive AI Evaluation
- Evryn

Each application page should answer:

- What domain does this apply to?
- Who is it for?
- What does it do?
- How does it express the WhyDive framework?
- Where should visitors go next?

### 7. Ecosystem

Purpose:

Prevent confusion among entities and domains.

Core sections:

- WhyDive as parent framework
- WhyDive Education as K-12 district application
- DLS as global data literacy standards application
- Chart-Ed Institute as DLS steward
- Chart-Ed as American K-12 teacher implementation
- Living Spiral Studio LLC as current legal parent
- Future entities

Required clarity:

- Chart-Ed is not WhyDive Education
- DLS is not K-12 only
- Chart-Ed Institute is not chart-ed.com
- WhyDive Education is not subordinate to DLS
- WhyDive.org is not a product site

### 8. About and Contact

Purpose:

Offer relational and legal clarity without centering the parent company over the framework.

Include:

- WhyDive is owned and operated by Living Spiral Studio LLC for now
- Same contact information as Living Spiral Studio LLC
- Clear note that WhyDive may become a separate business later, but is not separate yet
- Links to privacy and terms when those pages are created

## Midjourney Image Plan

Images should be generated after page structure is stable so visual prompts match actual content needs.

Use [Midjourney Image Prompt Plan](./midjourney-image-prompts.md) as the working prompt library for the first visual generation batch.

Needed image sizes:

- Hero background: wide desktop, 16:9 or 21:9
- Section banner: 16:9
- Essay/social card: 1.91:1
- Square card: 1:1
- Mobile hero crop: 4:5 or 9:16
- Open Graph image: 1200x630

Image families to create:

- Hero: contemplative depth, descent into understanding, light through water
- Foundations: framework architecture, thresholds, boundary lines, layered evidence
- Essays: writing, archive, marginalia, quiet study
- Whitepapers: formal publication, paper, citation, institutional seriousness
- Research: library, annotation, evidence trail, scholarly map
- Applications: branching framework, domains emerging from a central principle
- Ecosystem: constellation, spiral map, living architecture

## Design Watchouts

- Do not let the site drift into a district product pitch.
- Do not make the site feel like a startup landing page.
- Do not over-card the page.
- Do not bury the writing.
- Do not confuse WhyDive with Chart-Ed, DLS, or WhyDive Education.
- Do not imply WhyDive is already a separate legal company.
- Do not make the imagery too literal, corporate, or classroom-bound.
